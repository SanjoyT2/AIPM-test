The role

What an AI Implementation Executive
actually does

You go inside a real business and make AI run in the daily work. Someone in accounts stops doing four hours of copy-paste. A trader gets answers in Hindi on WhatsApp. That is the job.

This is the job

Sitting with the person who does the work, finding the step that wastes their day, and shipping something they keep using after you leave. You own it end to end and you can explain it live.

This is not the job

Building demos nobody runs twice. Writing prompts in a classroom with no client on the other side. Waiting for a certificate to make you employable.

Straight answers

Questions people actually ask

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Not to apply, and not to do the job. You need to be able to think clearly, talk to people, and be willing to sit with a business problem until it's solved. We train the AI part from zero.

What stream do I need to be from?

Any. We've trained people from commerce, arts, engineering and science. What we screen for is whether you can hold a conversation with a client and whether you finish things.

Is this a course or a job?

It starts as training and becomes work. You're deployed full-time inside one real business at a time, usually three to six months each, and the goal is you getting hired — not you collecting a certificate.

Where does this happen?

On client sites in Kolkata. This is in-person work inside real businesses, because that's the only place the job actually exists.

How long before I'm employable?

You're doing paid-for work on a live client site well before you'd finish a typical course. Your first deployment is your portfolio.

What happens after I apply?

You verify your number on WhatsApp, and we continue there — that's where your intake, your first brief and your coach live. Reply START and it begins.
